## Customer Expectations Before Arriving at a Restaurant

The guest experience begins well before a table is set. Today's diners want information instantly. They call to ask about hours, availability, menu options, dietary accommodations, wait times, and reservations. When these questions go unanswered or are handled inconsistently, uncertainty builds and frustration follows.

Answering questions before the visit is one of the most important steps in shaping a positive experience. Missed calls, long hold times, or rushed responses create doubt and can push diners toward another restaurant that feels easier to engage with.

Reducing uncertainty for diners is where AI voice assistants create immediate impact. heytruffle ensures every call is answered promptly, professionally, and accurately, giving guests confidence before they arrive. When diners feel informed and supported early, they're more likely to book, show up, and arrive with a positive mindset.

By meeting customer expectations upfront, restaurants eliminate friction before it has a chance to affect service or revenue.

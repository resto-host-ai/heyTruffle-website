# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Restaurant owners, operators, and multi-location teams evaluating how to answer every phone call without pulling staff away from guests. They often visit from a phone, between operational tasks, and need the value of the service to become clear immediately.

## Product Purpose

The site explains and generates leads for heytruffle, a fully managed English and Spanish voice AI service for restaurants. Success means a visitor can understand the offer, hear a host, review restaurant outcomes, and contact the team without waiting, troubleshooting, or managing a complex interface.

## Brand Personality

Warm, operational, confident. The experience should feel hospitality-led and human, while the product itself feels dependable and technically mature.

## Anti-references

Generic self-serve SaaS dashboards, decorative motion that delays content, black or blank loading states, effects that compete with reading, and interfaces that feel like another tool restaurant staff must configure.

## Design Principles

- Performance is part of trust: core content and controls must work immediately on a real phone.
- Show the service through restaurant outcomes and real host voices.
- Preserve warmth without relying on expensive visual effects.
- Keep the path from value to proof to contact direct.
- Progressive enhancement only: essential content and actions never depend on animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep content readable and usable with reduced motion, keyboard navigation, screen readers, and touch. Mobile and coarse-pointer devices receive a static, low-compositing presentation by default. Interactive media always requires and clearly responds to an explicit user action.
